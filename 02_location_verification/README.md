本文件夹用于核验机构宣传中所涉及的场地、位置及空间结构的真实性。所有分析基于公开地图、卫星影像及可公开获取的信息，不涉及个人信息或现场接触。

This folder is used to verify the authenticity of the venues, locations, and spatial structures mentioned in the organization's promotional materials.
All analyses are based on publicly available maps, satellite imagery, and other publicly accessible information, and do not involve personal information or on-site contact.
