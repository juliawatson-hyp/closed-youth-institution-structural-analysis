# Temporal Evolution of the Site (2022–2024)

## 1. Objective

This document analyzes observable physical changes at the site
associated with “Chongqing Yutu Youth Growth Education Base”
between 2022 and 2024 using historical satellite imagery and map data.

The purpose is to identify patterns of maintenance, functional adjustment,
and spatial stabilization over time.

---

## 2. Data Sources

- Google Earth historical imagery (2022–2024)
- Amap (Gaode) satellite imagery and location photos
- Publicly accessible map annotations

All data sources are open and require no restricted access.

---

## 3. Timeline of Observable Changes

### 3.1 Early Stage (circa 2022)

- Presence of a blue temporary plastic structure
  - Likely used for shelter or auxiliary storage
- Ground surface primarily cement-based
- No clearly marked parking spaces
- Site appearance consistent with transitional or semi-industrial use

---

### 3.2 Mid-to-Late Stage (2022–2024)

#### 3.2.1 Fire Access Markings (“Fire Lane” Grid)

- Yellow fire access grid lines were painted on the ground
- These markings persist across multiple image updates

**Interpretation:**
In China, fire access markings are typically associated with:
- Spaces expected to host people
- Areas subject to basic fire safety management
- Compliance signaling for inspections or routine oversight

Such markings usually imply:
- Anticipated regular human presence
- Defined responsibility for site safety
- Non-temporary usage of the space

---

#### 3.2.2 Removal of Temporary Structures and Landscaping

- The blue plastic structure was removed
- Four trees were planted in an orderly, symmetrical pattern

**Interpretation:**
- Removal of temporary facilities suggests functional stabilization
- Planned landscaping indicates long-term site expectations
- Tree planting is uncommon in short-term or transient operations

---

#### 3.2.3 Ground Surface and Parking Delineation

- Cement surface replaced with asphalt
- Corners and cluttered areas were cleared
- White parking lines were added

**Interpretation:**
These changes indicate:
- Regular vehicle usage
- Defined internal circulation
- Centralized management of space
- Ongoing maintenance investment

---

## 4. Structural Characteristics

- No traditional perimeter wall observed
- Site boundaries formed by:
  - Adjacent buildings
  - Warehouses
  - Industrial structures
- A single metal rotating gate serves as the primary access point

This configuration aligns with what is commonly described as
a “naturally enclosed site” rather than an open public space.

---

## 5. Analytical Implications

The combination of:
- Fire access markings
- Landscaping
- Surface upgrades
- Parking delineation

suggests the site transitioned from a flexible or mixed-use space
into a more stable, managed operational environment.

The changes occurred gradually rather than abruptly,
indicating sustained use rather than temporary occupation.

---

## 6. Analytical Boundaries

- No inference is made regarding internal activities
- No claims are made about institutional legality
- Analysis remains purely spatial and temporal
- Conclusions are provisional and evidence-based

# 场地时间演变分析（2022–2024）

## 一、分析目标

本文件基于 2022 至 2024 年的历史卫星影像，
分析与“重庆育途青少年成长教育基地”相关场地
在时间维度上的可观察变化。

重点在于识别：
- 场地是否被持续使用
- 是否存在功能收敛与规范化趋势
- 是否表现出长期运营预期

---

## 二、数据来源

- Google Earth 历史影像（2022–2024）
- 高德地图卫星图与实景照片
- 公开地图标注信息

所有数据均为公开来源。

---

## 三、主要时间节点变化

### 3.1 早期阶段（约 2022 年）

- 院内存在蓝色塑料棚
  - 属于临时性设施
- 地面为水泥材质
- 无明确停车位或功能分区
- 场地呈现出过渡性或混合用途特征

---

### 3.2 2022–2024 年期间的变化

#### 3.2.1 “消防通道”网格线的绘制

- 地面出现黄色消防通道网格
- 多期影像中持续存在

**分析说明：**
在中国语境下，消防通道标线通常意味着：
- 预期存在人员集中或活动
- 场地需要满足基础消防管理要求
- 使用方承担一定安全责任

这类标线一般不会用于
短期、无人或完全临时的场地。

---

#### 3.2.2 临时设施拆除与绿化

- 塑料棚被拆除
- 新种植 4 棵树木，排列规整

**分析说明：**
- 拆除临时结构表明用途趋于稳定
- 绿化行为暗示长期使用预期
- 树木种植通常不属于短期行为

---

#### 3.2.3 地面与停车管理变化

- 水泥地面更换为沥青
- 清理角落杂物
- 新增白色停车位标线

**分析说明：**
这些变化通常反映：
- 固定人员与车辆流动
- 明确的内部管理秩序
- 持续性的维护投入

---

## 四、空间结构特征

- 未观察到传统围墙
- 场地边界主要由周边建筑自然形成
- 单一金属旋转门作为主要出入口

该结构符合“自然围合型场地”的典型特征，
并非完全开放空间。

---

## 五、综合分析结论（非定性）

综合时间序列变化可推断：
- 场地并非临时占用
- 使用具有连续性
- 管理逐步规范化
- 存在长期运营预期

本结论仅限于空间与时间维度。

---

## 六、分析边界

- 不涉及内部管理或人员情况
- 不对机构性质作法律判断
- 结论可随新公开资料更新
